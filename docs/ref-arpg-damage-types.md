# ARPG Damage Types Reference

A reference document for damage types and their effects across action RPGs.

## Diablo 4

### Damage Types Overview

| Damage Type | Primary | Secondary | DoT Effect | CC Effect |
|-------------|---------|-----------|------------|-----------|
| Physical | Direct | Armor Break | Bleeding | None |
| Fire | Direct | Burn | Burn | None |
| Cold | Direct | Chill | None | Chill, Freeze |
| Lightning | Direct | Chain | None | Stun |
| Poison | Direct | Poison DoT | Poisoned | None |
| Shadow | Direct | Shadow DoT | Decay | None |
| Holy | Direct | Heal | None | None |

### Burn (Diablo 4)

- Fire damage can apply Burn DoT on hit
- Ticks every 1 second
- Scales with Fire damage bonuses
- Multiple Burn instances can stack
- Affected by damage over time stats

### Poisoned (Diablo 4)

- Poison damage applies Poisoned DoT
- Ticks every 1 second
- Scales with Poison damage bonuses
- Stacks multiple instances

### Decay (Diablo 4)

- Shadow damage applies Decay DoT
- Ticks every 1 second
- Hybrid build synergy

### Chill/Freeze (Diablo 4)

- Cold damage applies Chill (up to 30% slow)
- At 100%+ Chill, enemies become Frozen
- Frozen enemies take bonus damage from hits
- Bosses have higher Freeze threshold

### Overpower

- Separate mechanic from elemental damage
- Massive damage on proc
- Based on HP ratio (higher when low)
- Enhanced by Life/Overpower stats

## Path of Exile 2

### Damage Types Overview

| Damage Type | Primary | Secondary | DoT Effect | CC Effect |
|-------------|---------|-----------|------------|-----------|
| Physical | Direct | Bleeding, Maim | Bleeding | Maim |
| Fire | Direct | Ignite, Scorch | Ignite | None |
| Cold | Direct | Chill, Freeze | Chilled Ground | Freeze, Chill |
| Lightning | Direct | Shock, Sapped | Shocked Ground | Stun |
| Chaos | Direct | Poison, Despair | Poison | None |

### Ignite (PoE2)

- Fire damage inflicts Ignite
- 5 second duration DoT
- Creates burning ground effects (Brittle Ground)
- Reduced enemy regeneration
- Scales with Ailment Damage/Duration

### Bleeding (PoE2)

- Physical damage inflicts Bleeding
- Scales with area and physical damage
- Physical DoT type
- Enhanced by Physical damage modifiers

### Poison (PoE2)

- Chaos damage inflicts Poison
- Chaos DoT that stacks
- Scales with Poison/Potency stats
- Bypasses energy shield

### Shock (PoE2)

- Lightning damage inflicts Shock
- Increases damage taken by enemy (up to 150%)
- Creates Shocked Ground
- Higher magnitude = more damage increase

### Chill (PoE2)

- Cold damage inflicts Chill
- Up to 90% slow effect
- Creates Chilled Ground
- Leads to Freeze at high values

### Despair/Wither (PoE2)

- Chaos damage can inflict Despair
- Reduces enemy life/ES recovery rate
- Wither applies stacking debuff increasing Chaos damage taken

### Ailment Scaling Stats

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Ailment Application Rate | Chance to inflict ailments |
| Ailment Damage | DoT damage increase |
| Ailment Duration | Extends ailment duration |
| Ailment Magnitude | Non-DoT ailment effect |
